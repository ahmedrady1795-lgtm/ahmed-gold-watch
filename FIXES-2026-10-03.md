# Safety & Reliability Fixes — 2026-10-03

- MT5 position sizing no longer rounds a too-small risk size up to broker minimum lot. If minimum lot exceeds the configured risk budget, the trade is rejected.
- MT5 Bridge now pushes closed M1/M5/M15/H1 broker candles; fresh broker candles become the primary indicator source while MT5 is connected.
- Binance remains the fallback market-data proxy. Browser WebSocket URL corrected to the official USD-M Futures raw-stream path.
- Market/indicator refresh runs independently of the alerts toggle; the toggle now controls alerts only and its state persists locally.
- Telegram dedupe and last-signal state use D1 when available, reducing duplicate messages across serverless instances.
- MT5 execution attempts are rate-limited per signal to avoid repeated blocked-order spam.
- Health checks now validate `pricesReady`, identify the real candle source, and correctly show Backtest as Binance-backed without requiring Twelve Data.
- Indicator score rebalanced to reduce duplicated EMA influence and require more breakout/DI/M1 confirmation.
