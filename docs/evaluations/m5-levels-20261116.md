# M5 opportunity investigation — 2026-11-16

## Finding

The live M5 observer is primarily a momentum rule: 58% five-close momentum,
24% last-body direction, 12% close location and 6% optional M5 slope. It does not
require support/resistance proximity, an entry zone, invalidation or room for a
target. Its BUY/SELL caption must not be interpreted as validated pre-move edge.
Fixing missing signal windows did not establish prediction accuracy.

## Reproducible offline experiment

`node scripts/evaluate-m5-level-setups.cjs`

A frozen Biquote XAUUSD sample contains 300 M1 candles and corresponding M5 data.
The first 60% is the development segment; the remaining chronological 40% is a
small holdout. Parameters were fixed before inspecting its results; no parameter
search was performed. There can be one observation per M5 phase (open, middle,
close), matching live deduplication. These observations share candles and are
not independent trades. Prices use the observed minute open at issuance.
Only earlier closed candles enter features; later candles are used for scoring.

| Model | Development direction | Holdout direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing momentum rule | 27 / 71 | 28 / 55 |
| Experimental prior-level reaction | 6 / 13 | 4 / 10 |

For the experimental holdout: target first 2, invalidation first 3, ambiguous 0,
unresolved 5. The target check excludes the issuance minute and stops at the
current M5 close. Target results cannot be compared to the baseline's absent
fixed targets. No spread, commission, slippage or real fills were modeled.
This is a short diagnostic replay, not a statistically reliable win rate.

## Candidate implementation

`lib/sniper-m5-level-setup.ts` is an OFFLINE candidate, not imported by the live UI.
It requires a prior support/resistance level, closed-bar rejection or pullback,
price still near the level, a known target ahead, and explicit invalidation.
It rejects pure momentum, chasing, contradictory levels, gaps and stale data.
Causal tests cover both sides and future-bar exclusion.

## Decision

Do not replace the live observer with this candidate: neither direction results
nor target results establish an improvement. Preserve the failed experiment and
its immutable fixture rather than repeatedly tuning on the same holdout.
A future candidate needs a new multi-session evaluation including detection
lead time, excursion, target-before-invalidation and execution costs.
No Render deployment was made for this experiment.
